are positive. Explicit totals above99 are not capped. The fallback includes
standard and extended B covers together; it does not apply an editor's exclusive
cover presentation rule. Cover8/9/10 do not enter these four branches.

non-NULL predicate. The cap is **99**, unlike Long Lifeform's SetTo99 cap99.9.
