Operational lessons:

- FS1333 is reduced reconnaissance, not a synonym for full FS882. Ground calls,
  auditable inspections and full classification plots have different evidential
  completeness. Missing child rows do not establish assessed absence.
- Blank/unassessed, SQL NULL, empty text, observed zero, n/a and sentinels are
  not interchangeable. Paper marks have field-specific meanings; no general
  paper-to-SQL repair rule is established.
- Preserve units and depth origins. Mineral horizon depths start at uppermost
  mineral; FS1333 restricting depth starts at ground surface/top humus. Slope
  percent is not degrees; coordinates require explicit datum handling.
- Cover is projected area with overlap: species sums can exceed layer totals.
  Do not invent cover balancing or flatten taxa/layers/substrates/completeness
  when calculating reports.
- Collection requirements depend on project objectives. SIVI prose and Appendix
  mandatory-field lists differ (PDF200-202 versus210); form examples also span
  2008/2015 revisions. Do not strengthen Save validation or reinterpret historical
  values from the manual without current source and explicit workflow decisions.

Read-only R candidates include table/child export, collection-completeness
diagnostics, strata-aware summaries, explicit unit/datum checks and reproducible
report bundles. These are inferred opportunities, not specified APIs or verified
formulas.
