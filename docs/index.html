Workstation Installation & SHA-256 Verification

Verify every Phase 1 package hash locally before running on Ministry of Forests or research workstations.

Windows 10 / 11

  1. Download vpro-windows-amd64-installer.exe (or the portable .zip when installing without local admin rights).
  2. Verify the SHA-256 hash in PowerShell against SHA256SUMS.txt: 
    Get-FileHash .\vpro-windows-amd64-installer.exe -Algorithm SHA256
  3. If Windows Defender SmartScreen prompts on an unsigned Phase 1 build, confirm the SHA-256 matches SHA256SUMS.txt before selecting More info → Run anyway.

macOS Universal (VPro.app)

  1. Download vpro-macos-universal.dmg and verify its SHA-256 digest in Terminal: 
    shasum -a 256 vpro-macos-universal.dmg
  2. Mount the disk image and drag VPro.app to your Applications folder.
  3. Because Phase 1 builds use ad-hoc signing without Apple notarization, Control-click VPro.app in Applications and select Open on first launch after verifying the checksum.

Linux (amd64 & arm64)

  1. Verify the downloaded package against SHA256SUMS.txt: 
    sha256sum -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS.txt
  2. Run the AppImage directly or install the Debian package: 
    chmod +x vpro-linux-amd64.AppImage && ./vpro-linux-amd64.AppImage
sudo apt install ./vpro-linux-amd64.deb
  3. Requires GTK4 and WebKitGTK 6.0 runtime libraries; pass --appimage-extract-and-run when FUSE is unavailable.

#doc-key.rsmr_9_4

Relative Soil Moisture Regime (rSMR 0–8) Decision Key

Step Diagnostic Criterion / Reference Scale If YesIf No
1 Standing water continuously above soil surface during growing season? 8 — Hydric Go to Step 2
2 Water table or prominent gleying at <= 30 cm throughout most of growing season (or wetland peat > 40 cm)? 7 — Subhydric Go to Step 3
3 Permanent growing-season seepage or distinct mottles/gleying within 30–60 cm on lower/toe/depression positions? 6 — Hygric Go to Step 4
4 Temporary seepage (< 60–100 cm) OR deep medium/fine receiving lower/toe slope? 5 — Subhygric Go to Step 5 (Upland Water-Deficit Key 9.4)
5 Very shallow lithic soil (< 20 cm to bedrock) on crest/upper steep warm aspect, OR > 70% coarse fragments in sand? 0 (Very Xeric) or 1 (Xeric) Go to Step 6
6 Shallow soil (20–50 cm) OR coarse sandy/skeletal (> 35–70% CF) on shedding crest/upper slope? 2 — Subxeric (or 3 — Submesic if medium texture / middle slope) 4 — Mesic (deep >= 100 cm medium-textured middle/level normal site)
LMH25 Key 9.6 LMH25 (2023) p. 39
#doc-key.rsnr_9_6

Relative Soil Nutrient Regime (rSNR A–E) Decision Key

Step Diagnostic Criterion / Reference Scale If YesIf No
1 Wetland Sphagnum bog peat (Of, pH < 4.5) OR very shallow quartzitic/granitic soil with thick Mor humus + white Ae? A — Very Poor Go to Step 2
2 Mor or Mormoder humus, continuous Ae (>= 2 cm), coarse acidic parent material, no Ah and no seepage? B — Poor Go to Step 3
3 Moder or thin Humimor humus, weak/absent Ae (< 2 cm), medium-textured non-calcareous till/fluvial without Ah? C — Medium Go to Step 4
4 Mullmoder/Vermimull OR Ah horizon (2–10 cm) OR telluric seepage / dark base-rich parent material? D — Rich Go to Step 5
5 Vermimull humus with dark friable Ah >= 10 cm, alluvial/carbonate enrichment, or Minerotrophic rich fen/seepage? E — Very Rich (use F for Saline/Alkaline) Re-check humus form, Ah thickness, and seepage

SITE 20 LMH25 (2023) p. 34 Edaphic & Hydrologic Regime
See Decision Key (key.rsmr_9_4) #field-site.moisture_regime

Relative Soil Moisture Regime (rSMR)

Relative growing-season soil water availability within a biogeoclimatic subzone/variant (0 = very xeric through 8 = hydric).

SQLite / VP08 Column: MoistureRegime · Unit / Type: 0–8 Ordinal Scale (Key 9.3 / 9.4)

  • Evaluated via LMH25 Key 9.3 (wet/water-table soils) and Key 9.4 (upland soils from slope position, soil depth, coarse fragments, texture, and seepage).
  • Paired with rSNR (A–E) on the 9 x 5 BEC Edatopic Grid and transformed via climr CMD into Actual SMR (aSMR).
0 — Very Xeric — very shallow/lithic or steep south coarse talus
1 — Xeric — shallow or very coarse shedding upper slopes
2 — Subxeric — moderately dry coarse/shallow soils
3 — Submesic — slightly drier than zonal normal
4 — Mesic — normal water balance reflecting regional climate
5 — Subhygric — temporary growing-season seepage or deep fine receiving soil
6 — Hygric — permanent seepage / gleying within 30–60 cm
7 — Subhydric — water table at or near surface (< 30 cm) most of year
8 — Hydric — standing water above soil surface throughout growing season

Relative Soil Nutrient Regime (rSNR)

B — Poor — suboligotrophic (Hemimor/Mormoder, prominent Ae)
C — Medium — mesotrophic (Moder/Humimor, balanced base status)
D — Rich — permesotrophic (Leptomoder/Vermimull, Ah present or seepage)
E — Very Rich — eutrophic (Mull, dark Ah >= 10 cm, carbonate/telluric seepage)

Organic Horizon Upper & Lower Depth (LMH25 Sign Rule)

Directional depth boundaries in centimetres obeying the Canadian System of Soil Classification & LMH25 zero-datum convention.
